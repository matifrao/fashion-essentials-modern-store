Frequently Asked Questions About Hijabs

What types of hijabs are available?

Fashion Essentials offers a selection of hijabs and modest fashion essentials in different colors, styles, and fabrics. Our collection is designed to provide versatile options for everyday wear and different occasions.

How do I choose the right hijab?

Consider the occasion, fabric, color, coverage, and the type of outfit you want to wear. A versatile color and comfortable fabric can be a good choice for everyday use, while more refined fabrics and colors can work well for formal occasions.

Which hijab color should I choose for everyday wear?

Neutral colors such as black, beige, cream, brown, grey, and navy are versatile choices that can complement many outfits. You can also choose colors that match or contrast with your existing wardrobe.

How should I care for my hijab?

Always follow the care instructions provided for the specific fabric. In general, gentle washing and careful handling can help preserve the fabric, color, and shape of your hijab.

More About Hijabs

Purpose and Rules

What is the purpose of wearing a hijab?

It fulfills a religious commandment for modesty in Islam, and it serves as a visible symbol of Muslim identity.

When can a girl take off her hijab?

In private spaces around immediate family members (Mahrams), and in front of other women and young children.

Cultural Context

Does Pakistan wear hijab?

The practice is highly common but varies by individual preference. Women choose various styles including dupattas, abayas, or headscarves.

Practical Styling & Wear

What do girls wear under a hijab?

An under-scarf or bonnet cap keeps hair securely in place. Scrunchies add volume, while magnets or pins secure the fabric.

What hijab fabrics do you offer?

Lightweight Chiffon, Stretchy Jersey, Breathable Cotton, Premium Silk and Modal.

Which hijab is best for daily wear?

Jersey hijabs do not require pins and resist slipping. Cotton crinkle hijabs stay secure and require no ironing.

Can I find hijabs for special occasions?

Yes, luxury options include satin, silk, and embellished chiffon.
